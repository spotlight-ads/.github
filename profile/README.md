# Spotlight

An ad network for the Solana ecosystem. Advertisers reach people on the Solana apps and sites they already use, and publishers earn from the ads they show.

Spotlight is in development and serves no ads yet. When it does, staff review every ad before it serves, with no fingerprinting and no third-party cookies.

- Site: spotlightads.app, not live yet
- X: [@spotlightads_ai](https://x.com/spotlightads_ai)

## Repositories

There are no public repositories here yet. Publishers will place ads with a small embed client, `@spotlightads/client`, and its React wrapper, `@spotlightads/react`. Neither is published yet.

## Security

Report a vulnerability privately from the affected repository's **Security** tab, or through [this form](https://github.com/spotlight-ads/.github/security/advisories/new). Please don't open a public issue.

Spotlight is made by [Aerosol](https://www.aerosol.build).
